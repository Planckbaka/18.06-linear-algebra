# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and intellectually rigorous learning community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive academic and open-source environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward learners at every mathematical background level
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive mathematical and editorial feedback
* Citing definitions, theorems, dimensions, and counterexamples precisely when discussing technical errata
* Focusing on what is best for clarity, rigor, and pedagogical intuition

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Condescension or gatekeeping directed at newcomers asking sincere mathematical questions
* Plagiarizing copyrighted textbook text or unattributed third-party materials

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening amaintainer-flagged issue or contacting the repository owner via GitHub (`@Planckbaka`). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
